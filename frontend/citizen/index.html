Child Safety Reporting

The CyberTipline is the place to report child sexual exploitation.

If you believe you or someone you know is a victim of exploitation, we are here to help.

Before you report
  • You can report anonymously if you prefer.
  • We do not require you to re-upload distressing material or relive the incident unnecessarily.
  • Urgent danger reports can be routed immediately to 112 and local child protection services.
